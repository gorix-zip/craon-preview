Design system

Cray. Craon's design system.

Two faces, both grotesk, nine type steps, one tungsten that marks what is live, a framed tag, and a raised control built from two inner lights. Film-black, ink, hairlines, no glass, no glow. Everything on this page is the live CSS the site uses, not a picture of it.

Brand

The mark and the lockup are craon.ai's own; the system does not redraw them.

Colour

Film-black is the page. Tungsten marks what is live: the editor's playhead and its ready dot, and focus. It is never a background and never body copy. A section may opt into the light surface with data-theme=light and every token under it flips, including the button lights.

  • --color-bgthe page
  • --color-bg-2cards and raised material
  • --color-bg-3pressed chips, secondary buttons
  • --color-bg-deepfields and wells
  • --color-fgtype and hairlines at full strength
  • --color-fg-2body copy on a dark surface
  • --color-fg-3eyebrows, metadata
  • --color-fg-loghistory and logs only: the chat's checks and collapsed lines
  • --color-linehairlines
  • --color-brandone control per viewport, and what is live
  • --color-cutthe removed state only

Controls

One recipe, four fills. The relief is two inner lights: the top edge catches light, the bottom edge falls into shadow. A 1px contact shadow keeps it on the surface, a soft drop lifts it. Hover steps the fill and deepens the drop, and adds a ring; nothing moves. Press moves the light inward. Corners are 8px, never a pill.

Chips and fields

A chip is flat at rest and only the selected one is raised, so a row of chips has one object in it. The small chip takes the tag's corner and sits inside a preview well. A field is a well: it sits below the surface, where a button sits above it.

The field: the well with its submit inside; the ring lives on the wrapper.

Nothing you type leaves the page.

The hero's card over the footage: opaque bg-2, r12, a 1px line-2 edge that does not thin; a keyboard focus in the field puts focus's tungsten on the card.

Surfaces

The card is flat material with a hairline. Media sits in a deeper well with a title bar. The readout is four recessed cells, two by two. The aspect mark is an empty frame in a format's own ratio. Corners are one family, 6 · 8 · 12: tag, control, card and media. There is no pill.

Editor

The product, rebuilt from its screenshot in these parts: the bar, the chat, the transport, the timeline, the tools and the export sheet. Only what works or what a chapter answers is drawn. The frame is bg-2, the chat and track heads bg-3, the viewer the deepest surface. Tungsten is the playhead and the ready dot, nothing else; the removed state is the cut red. Clips, words and joins take the tag corner.

Capability cards

Six of one size on one grid, three across on desk and two under 1024: the picture is the only box and the h3 is the only line under it. A card is a still from the footage or one lane of the timeline, at the same 16:9 either way. Each plays once in view and replays on hover or focus; the card itself never moves. Under reduced motion it rests on its finished frame.

  • One look across every shot
  • Story that opens on the hook

Section mark

Every section opens with a tag over its ruler. On desk (861 and up) the tag travels along the ruler as the mark crosses the viewport, from the left end as it enters at the bottom to the right end once it is 48px under the nav. At rest, on a phone and under reduced motion it sits at col 1. Scroll this one to see it.

Who it's for

Space and rhythm

A 4px scale. Sections breathe at clamp(96px, 11vw, 170px); the content measure is 1320 with a fluid gutter. The reveal is 22px and 620ms on one easing, cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1), staggered 70ms.
