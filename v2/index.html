Before you upload

Four questions. Plain answers.

Do I need to know how to edit?
No. Describe the video you want, and Craon makes the editing decisions. You review its plan, approve it or change it, and refine the result in plain words.
Will Craon cut something I wanted to keep?
Not without you seeing it. Craon shows an edit plan before it cuts anything, and you can ask for changes afterward, like "keep the laugh" or "put the second take back."
Can I change the edit after Craon makes it?
Yes. Ask for changes the way you would brief an editor: "open on the laugh," "make it warmer," or "cut a vertical version." Each change can be kept or undone.
Does it work with Hinglish?
Yes. Craon removes Hinglish filler words, like "matlab" and "basically," along with English ones, so mixed-language speech comes out clean.
